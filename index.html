Platform features

Everything a deathcare business needs in one place.



Multi-tenant billing

Isolated billing per tenant with Stripe integration, invoice generation, and payment tracking built for the deathcare industry.



Case management

Track arrangements, cremations, and pre-need cases from first call through final disposition with full audit history.



Financial reporting

Revenue summaries, outstanding balances, and case-level financial detail — the reports your business actually needs.



Secure by design

Supabase-backed auth, tenant isolation at the data layer, and SOX-aware access controls built in from the start.



Offline-capable desktop app

A JavaFX desktop client that works without internet, built for rural funeral homes with unreliable connectivity. Statements sync automatically when the connection resumes.



Per-tenant branding

Every funeral home gets their own logo, name, and address on printed statements. Your brand, not ours.



Self-service onboarding

Sign up, choose a plan, and start working. No manual provisioning, no waiting. Stripe-gated activation gets you running in minutes.

Built in the open

Purpose-built for an underserved industry.

Eternatel is being built by a software engineer with firsthand understanding of the deathcare industry's unique needs. The technical design is fully documented and open for review.
